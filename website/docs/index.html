What this product is

AI Job Search drives an unmodified ai-job-search checkout through Claude Code. The desktop app never reimplements slash commands — it supervises runs, shows approvals, and mirrors tracker/documents state from disk.

InstallmacOS app, Claude Code, Python, and LaTeX. First runPick a workspace, finish setup, check dependencies. Discover & rankFind roles and shortlist what fits. ApplyDraft in Studio, compile PDFs, stay in control.
Local-first. Tracker CSV, seen jobs, archives, and tailored documents stay in your workspace. Credentials stay with Claude Code — not inside the app.
