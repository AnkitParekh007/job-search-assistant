# Flagship screen recording

16:9. Aim for 6–9 minutes. Record the shipping Apple Silicon app on the fictional `demo/` workspace. Do not use a personal job search.

Title (pick one, no growth adjective):

- I got tired of auto-apply bots, so I built a local job-search app
- A local job-search app that will not apply for you

Thumbnail and description are in the sibling files. Burned-in captions are optional. Upload an SRT from this script if you add one later.

## Do not say

No interviews won, no reply rates, no “beat the ATS,” no named real employer as a customer, no “works on Windows” as a download. The Nordlicht score on screen is sample data. Say that once.

## Shot list

| Time | On screen | Say |
|------|-----------|-----|
